- Players and teams covered: all 15 Eastern teams (ATL, BOS, BKN, CHA, CHI, CLE, DET, IND, MIA, MIL, NY, ORL, PHI, TOR, WSH). Starters, preseason standouts, coach-confirmed roles and opener injury status for each rotation.
- Openers (from CBS team pages, fetched 19 Oct 2026): **Tue 20 Oct** BOS @ DET, PHI @ NY. **Wed 21 Oct** ATL @ ORL, MIL @ WSH, CHI @ TOR, MIN @ MIA, CHA @ BKN, IND @ NOP. **Thu 22 Oct** CLE @ PHI.

### How to read the stat tables

- **Source for every table:** the CBS Sports team stats page for that team (`https://www.cbssports.com/nba/teams/<ABBR>/<slug>/stats/`). These pages have no publish date. All were fetched 19 Oct 2026.
- **Season type:** the pages are titled "2026-27" but do not label the season type. The header still shows last season's record. The regular season starts 20 Oct, so every 2026-27 game in these tables is a **preseason** game. Each table's game count matches that team's preseason schedule.
- **Columns:** GP/GS are games played and started. **MPG and PPG are per game.** FG, FT, 3PM, REB, AST, STL, BLK and TO are **preseason totals**. "n/s" means the page did not show it (ATL has no GS column).
- **Accuracy:** the figures were extracted by a summarising tool. Spot checks against single-game recaps matched: Duren's two games add up to 14-18 FG and 10-12 FT, Leonard's to 27 pts, and Giannis matches Rotowire's per-game averages. Errors in individual cells are still possible.
- **Context:** preseason minutes are low because starters usually played only a half.
- **Single-game lines** are from the Rotowire notes carried on each CBS team page (`https://www.cbssports.com/nba/teams/<ABBR>/<slug>/`), cited by the note's date. "n/r" means the note did not report that stat.
