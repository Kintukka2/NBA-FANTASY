## How to read this

- **Stat-line format.** `min / pts / FG / 3PT / FT / reb / ast / stl / blk / TO`. "n/r" means the source did not report that figure.
- **Game type.** Every line is a **single preseason game** unless it is labelled **"preseason per-game"**.
- **Where the lines come from.** Most player lines are Rotowire notes shown on each CBS Sports team page. Those pages are cited as **[R-XXX]**, with the note date given. Notes are usually dated the day after the game. The URL and fetch date for each code are in the Sources list at the end.
- **No full preseason box scores could be opened.** ESPN story and stats pages rendered empty. ESPN's JSON API, RealGM and Proballers returned 403. CBS box-score pages were cut off mid-game. Fox Sports' "preseason" team page actually showed 2025-26 regular-season data. So full preseason averages with FG/FT/TO are mostly **could not confirm**. Turnovers are missing from most notes.
- **[YSR]** is a syndicated Stadium Rant roundup on Yahoo (17 Oct). Treat it as an aggregator, not a box score.
- **Official injury report.** The NBA's official report for 20 Oct games is first issued at 5 p.m. ET on 19 Oct (21:00 UTC), after this search. Injury status below comes from the CBS Sports injury page, fetched 19 Oct, with each entry's "updated" date, plus team and beat reporting.
